Think-aloud session

Thank you for helping us evaluate our game, The Collector. In this puzzle game set in 2176, you will be welcomed to the Central Archive, where nothing is as it seems and everything is out of place. You will solve riddles to find the missing pieces of the Archive and restore order to the world.

You can help us evaluate the game in one of two ways: a “think-aloud” session, or a questionnaire.

If you don’t mind sharing recording data (screen, mic, and optionally webcam) with us, then the think-aloud method will likely be the fastest/easiest to complete. The checkboxes below allow you to record your gameplay and then save the recordings to your device when you finish playing. We ask that you then use a cloud storage service like OneDrive to share these files with us. If you don’t have access to a cloud storage service like OneDrive, please let us know and we can create a link for you to upload the files to.

If you’d rather not share recording data, please feel free to complete a short questionnaire after playing the game instead. We request that think-aloud participants do not participate in the questionnaire and vice versa (choose one).

Please note that regardless of the evaluation method you choose, the information you share with us will only be accessed by students and staff at the University of Melbourne for educational and research purposes, and it will be handled in compliance with the Australian Privacy Act (1988) and the University of Melbourne’s Privacy Policy.
