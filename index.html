Thank you for helping us evaluate our game, The Collector. In this puzzle game set in 2176, you will be welcomed to the Central Archive, where nothing is as it seems and everything is out of place. You will solve riddles to find the missing pieces of the Archive and restore order to the world.

You can choose how much data you'd like to share with us, but we would like to record your screen, webcam, and microphone so we can see how you play and hear your thoughts. You can stop the recording at any time and choose what information you share. The gameplay shouldn't take much longer than 10 minutes (unless you want it to).

You may wish to assist us by completing a short questionnaire about the game instead of sharing recording data with us (please don't do both).
